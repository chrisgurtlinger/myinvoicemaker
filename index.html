Frequently asked questions

What does the free version include?

The free version lets you have up to 2 invoices at a time, export up to 2 invoices to PDF in total, keep up to 5 clients, and add up to 5 line items to an invoice. Exports use the System Default font.

What does the Lifetime Unlock include?

It's a single one-time purchase, with no subscription and no recurring charges. It removes all of the free limits and also turns on your logo, any font, a custom background banner on your invoices, and a custom app background color.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open any screen that offers the unlock (for example Profile, then Unlock Lifetime Access) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone and iPad) or Google account (Android) that you used to buy it. Your purchase works on every device signed in to that account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them. On iPhone or iPad, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open the Google Play Store, go to your order history, and request a refund there.

Where is my invoice data stored?

Only on your device. The app has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details. Because the data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your invoices, so share your PDFs somewhere safe first.

How do I send an invoice?

Open an invoice and tap Generate & Share PDF. You can send it through Mail, Messages, AirDrop, save it to Files, or use any app on the share sheet. Tap Preview Invoice first to check how it will look.

How do I add my logo or banner?

Go to the Profile tab. Choose a square image for your logo (at least 300×300 pixels works best) and a wide image for the banner (about 1600×340 pixels). These need the Lifetime Unlock.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. My Invoice Maker Pro runs on iPhone and iPad, and also on Android phones.
