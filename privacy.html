Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

My Invoice Maker Pro ("the app") is made by Chris Gurtlinger. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it doesn't share any data with third parties.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter, including your business profile, logo and banner images, clients, invoices, and settings, is stored only on your device. Invoice PDFs are created on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them, for example by email or AirDrop.

Photos

If you pick a logo or banner image, the app reads only the photo you select. It doesn't access the rest of your photo library.

Purchases

The optional Lifetime Unlock is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We don't receive or store your payment details. We only learn that a purchase happened, through standard reports from Apple or Google.

Backups

Your device's own backup system (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, or Google backup on Android) may include the app's data if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by you and your device settings, and we have no access to those backups.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your device. Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
